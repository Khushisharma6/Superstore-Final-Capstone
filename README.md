# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Superstore dataset using Microsoft Power BI to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, regional performance, and business trends.

The goal of this capstone project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, analysis, visualization, and interactive dashboards.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profitability
* Identify high-performing product categories
* Compare sales across different regions
* Analyze monthly sales trends
* Understand customer segment performance
* Compare profitability across categories and regions
* Present actionable business insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **Power Query**
* **DAX**
* **Data Visualization**
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### Page 2 — Customer & Profit Analysis

The second dashboard focuses on customer segments and profitability:

* Sales by Customer Segment
* Profit by Category
* Profit by Region
* Region filter/slicer
* Key Business Insights

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

### Sales Analysis

Analyzed sales performance across product categories and geographical regions.

### Profitability Analysis

Compared profit across categories and regions to identify areas of stronger and weaker profitability.

### Customer Segment Analysis

Analyzed sales contribution from Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

### Time-Series Analysis

Examined monthly sales trends to understand changes in business performance over time.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* Technology is an important contributor to overall sales performance.
* Customer segments contribute differently to total sales and can be targeted with segment-specific strategies.
* Regional profitability varies, highlighting opportunities for improving regional performance.
* Comparing sales with profit helps identify areas where higher revenue does not necessarily result in higher profitability.
* Time-based sales analysis helps identify changes and trends in business performance.
